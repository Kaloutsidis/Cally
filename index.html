01

Pick up where you left off.

Sign in with Apple, Google or an email code. Your entries, income sources and currency sync across your iPhones.

02

Keep going, even offline.

Once you’re signed in, Cally keeps a copy on your iPhone. Log personal income and expenses offline; your changes sync when you’re back online.

03

Keep a copy of your own.

Keep a JSON backup of your personal entries and settings. Import it again later, or bring in a backup from the original Cally web app. Premium also adds CSV export.

04

Set it up your way.

Choose your currency symbol, add your own income sources and turn on an optional Face ID or passcode lock. No bank connection required.

05

A goal you can share.

Together brings a shared budget or savings goal into its own space. Invite people and add group entries, while your personal calendar stays private.
