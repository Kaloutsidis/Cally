Support

Help for Cally on iPhone (iOS 17 or later)

Need help?

Questions, bugs and ideas are all welcome. Cally is made by one developer, Nikos Kaloutsidis.

Email support@cally.website

How do I sign in?

Cally needs an account so your entries are saved and sync between your iPhones. On the welcome screen, choose one:

  • Continue with Apple signs you in with your Apple ID.
  • Continue with Google opens Google’s sign-in page.
  • Continue with email: enter your email address and tap Send code. Enter the 6-digit code using AutoFill when offered, Cally’s Paste button, or the keyboard.

The email’s Copy code & open Cally button opens a page where you tap Copy code, then Open Cally. Back in Cally, tap Paste. If the code field isn’t open, choose Continue with email, enter the same address, then tap I already have a code.

There’s no password, and Cally never asks for your phone number. Your first sign-in creates your account.

I didn’t get the sign-in code

  • Check your spam or junk folder.
  • Wait a minute. Emails can take a moment to arrive.
  • Still nothing? Tap Send a new code (it’s available a minute after the last one) and use the newest code.
  • If the address has a typo, tap Use a different email.

Each code works for 10 minutes. If an older email has an 8-digit code, tap Send a new code and use the new 6-digit code. Never share it with anyone: Cally will never ask you for it.

How do I use Cally on a new or second iPhone?

Install Cally on that iPhone and sign in the same way as before, with the same Apple ID, Google account or email address. Your entries, income sources and currency come across, and changes sync both ways.

Cally keeps working offline. Changes you make without a connection sync when you’re back online.

What does Hide My Email do?

The first time you continue with Apple, Apple lets you choose Hide My Email. If you do, Cally gets a private relay address from Apple instead of your real email address. Cally works the same either way.

To reach the same account on another iPhone, continue with Apple there too, with the same Apple ID.

What does Premium include?

Free includes personal income and expense logging, the month overview, account sync and JSON backups, with ads. Premium removes ads and adds Together, budgets, year views, more custom categories and income sources, a custom tab bar, CSV export and extra appearance choices.

If Premium ends, your existing personal entries, categories, sources and budgets are kept. Features that require Premium become locked; your data is not deleted.

What does Premium cost?

In the Greek App Store, Premium is €2.99 per month or €19.99 per year. These are direct paid plans: payment starts when you confirm the purchase.

An optional seven-day free trial of the monthly plan may be offered to eligible Free users after using Cally on three different calendar days and closing the paid plans. It is offered once per Cally account on this device, with Not now available. The offer states the monthly renewal price before you choose it. Apple confirms eligibility; one introductory offer is allowed per subscription group.

Prices are localized by the App Store and may differ by country. Cally shows the current offer and renewal price before you continue, and Apple asks you to confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel.

How do I cancel or restore Premium?

  • Cancel or change a plan: open Settings → Manage subscription in Cally, or your iPhone’s Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Cally.
  • Avoid the next charge: cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current subscription period ends. Apple shows your renewal date.
  • Restore: use Restore purchases in Settings or Restore on the Premium page, signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Deleting the app or your Cally account does not cancel Premium. If Premium was purchased with another Apple Account, manage its billing with that account. Apple handles payment and refunds.

How does Together work?

Open ☰ → Together to create a shared budget or savings goal, or to join with a code. Choose the display name you want the group to see. Only entries added to that group are shared; your personal calendar and email address stay separate.

Creating a group requires Premium. Each member with Premium covers one member without it, and a group needs at least one Premium member. A group can have up to 10 members. If the Premium balance is no longer sufficient, the group keeps its history but cannot accept new entries until the balance is restored.

Share the invite code only with people you want in the group. When you leave, your entries stay under your chosen name; the group’s owner can delete them. If you delete your Cally account, the group entries you added are removed too. The last member leaving deletes the group.

Together needs an internet connection. Personal entries can still be logged offline and sync when you reconnect. Personal JSON and CSV backups do not include Together group data.

Why am I seeing ads?

The free version can show a banner above the tab bar. An occasional full-screen ad can appear after you save an entry and close the day, after your first 24 hours and no more than once every five minutes or three times a day. Premium removes ads.

Cally requests non-personalized ads. It does not send your personal or Together entries, email address, name or Cally account ID to the ad SDK. Google still processes device, advertising, interaction and diagnostic information to provide the ads.

Where required, Google asks for consent before ads can be requested. If applicable, open Settings → Ad privacy choices to revisit those choices. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete account and confirm. This permanently deletes your account and personal entries, along with your Together memberships and the group entries you added. Other iPhones signed in to it are signed out the next time Cally connects on them, and their copies are deleted. If you signed in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm once more. Cally then attempts to revoke its Sign in with Apple access. Your Cally account is deleted even if that revocation fails.

This can’t be undone, so export a backup first if you might want your entries later. Deleting the app doesn’t delete your account. If you sign in again after deleting your account, you start with a new, empty one. Cancel Premium separately through Apple; account deletion does not stop subscription billing.

What happens when I sign out?

Go to Settings → Sign out. Cally deletes its copy of your entries from this iPhone. Your account isn’t affected: everything that has synced stays in it, and signing in again brings it back. Your other iPhones stay signed in.

If some changes haven’t synced yet, Cally tries to send them first. If it can’t, for example while you’re offline, it warns you before signing out, because those changes would be lost.

How do I bring in my data from the Cally web app?

  1. In the Cally web app, open Overview, scroll to Backup and tap Save backup. Choose Save to Files, for example to iCloud Drive. (If Safari downloads it instead, it’s in Files → Downloads.)
  2. In the Cally iPhone app, go to Settings → Import and pick that file.

Your income and expense entries and your income sources come across and are added to your account, so they sync to your other iPhones. Nothing changes in the web app. Import also accepts backups exported from the Cally iPhone app, and importing the same file twice won’t create duplicates.

How do I change the currency?

Go to Settings → Currency and pick one. This changes the symbol only. Amounts aren’t converted, so an entry of €50 will show as $50. The currency is saved to your account, so it’s the same on all your iPhones.

How do I turn on the lock?

Go to Settings and turn on Lock with Face ID. On iPhones with Touch ID it says Touch ID; without either, passcode. Confirm once, and from then on Cally asks every time it opens or comes back from the background. It also hides your amounts in the app switcher.

Your iPhone needs a passcode for the lock to work. If Face ID doesn’t recognize you, you can use your iPhone passcode. The lock is set separately on each iPhone. To turn it off, use the same switch.

How do I delete entries?

  • One entry: tap its day, then tap the × next to the entry. You can undo right after. It’s removed on all your iPhones.
  • All entries: go to Settings → Delete all entries and confirm. This removes every entry and the income sources you added, on all your iPhones, but keeps your account, custom categories, budgets and Together groups. Export a backup first if you might need it.
  • Everything, including your account: see How do I delete my account?

Files you exported are separate copies. Delete those in the Files app.

How do I contact you?

Email support@cally.website. If something isn’t working, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Cally version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and how you sign in (Apple, Google or email).

Support messages are forwarded through ImprovMX to my Gmail inbox. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Please don’t attach a backup file unless I ask for one. It contains all your entries.

Still using Cally in your browser? Open the original web app to keep using it or save a backup. Your web entries stay in the browser where you created them.
