Support

Help for Cally on iPhone (iOS 17 or later)

Need help?

Questions, bugs and ideas are all welcome. Cally is made by one developer, Nikos Kaloutsidis.

Email support@cally.website

How do I sign in?

Cally needs an account so your entries are saved and sync between your iPhones. On the welcome screen, choose one:

  • Continue with Apple signs you in with your Apple ID.
  • Continue with Google opens Google’s sign-in page.
  • Continue with email: enter your email address and tap Send code. Enter the 6-digit code using AutoFill when offered, Cally’s Paste button, or the keyboard.

The email’s Copy code & open Cally button opens a page where you tap Copy code, then Open Cally. Back in Cally, tap Paste. If the code field isn’t open, choose Continue with email, enter the same address, then tap I already have a code.

There’s no password, and Cally never asks for your phone number. Your first sign-in creates your account.

I didn’t get the sign-in code

  • Check your spam or junk folder.
  • Wait a minute. Emails can take a moment to arrive.
  • Still nothing? Tap Send a new code (it’s available a minute after the last one) and use the newest code.
  • If the address has a typo, tap Use a different email.

Each code works for 10 minutes. If an older email has an 8-digit code, tap Send a new code and use the new 6-digit code. Never share it with anyone: Cally will never ask you for it.

How do I use Cally on a new or second iPhone?

Install Cally on that iPhone and sign in the same way as before, with the same Apple ID, Google account or email address. Your entries, income sources and currency come across, and changes sync both ways.

Cally keeps working offline. Changes you make without a connection sync when you’re back online.

What does Hide My Email do?

The first time you continue with Apple, Apple lets you choose Hide My Email. If you do, Cally gets a private relay address from Apple instead of your real email address. Cally works the same either way.

To reach the same account on another iPhone, continue with Apple there too, with the same Apple ID.

What does Premium include?

Free includes income and expense logging, the month overview, three recurring entries, a daily reminder, the Today widget, Siri and Shortcuts, month recap sharing, dark mode, account sync and JSON backups, with ads.

Premium removes ads and adds safe-to-spend estimates and a month-end forecast, bill reminders, on-device receipt scan, Budget, Month and Lock Screen widgets, up to 50 recurring entries, Together group creation, budgets, year views, more categories and income sources, custom tabs, CSV export and extra appearance choices.

Open ☰ More → Cally Premium to see the paid plans. A locked feature first explains what it adds; choose See Premium to continue, or Not now. The main header is just CALLY.

If Premium ends, your entries and existing budgets, categories and sources are kept. Premium features lock; your data is not deleted.

What does Premium cost?

In the Greek App Store, the direct paid plans are €2.99 per month and €19.99 per year. Payment starts when you confirm the purchase; these primary plans do not force a trial.

A one-time Lifetime purchase is €49.99 in Greece. It is under the small See other offers disclosure below Monthly and Yearly, and does not renew. If you already have a subscription, buying Lifetime does not automatically cancel it: cancel that subscription separately through Apple.

An optional seven-day free trial of the monthly plan may be offered separately to eligible Free users after three different active calendar days and an explicit close of the paid plans. It is offered once per Cally account on that device, with Not now available. It clearly states the monthly renewal price. Apple confirms eligibility; one introductory redemption is allowed per subscription group. Cancelling Apple’s purchase sheet alone does not trigger this offer.

Prices quoted here are for 2 November 2026. Prices are localized and may differ by country. Cally and Apple’s confirmation sheet show the current price and terms before you buy. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled; Lifetime is a single payment.

How do I cancel or restore Premium?

  • Cancel or change a subscription: open ☰ More → Account → Manage, or your iPhone’s Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Cally.
  • Avoid the next charge: cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current subscription period ends. Apple shows your renewal date.
  • Restore a subscription or Lifetime: use ☰ More → Account → Restore, or Restore on the Premium page, with the Apple Account that bought it.

Deleting the app or your Cally account does not cancel a subscription. Manage billing with the Apple Account that bought it. Lifetime has no recurring charge. Apple handles payments and refunds.

How do recurring entries work?

Open ☰ More → Recurring, or use Repeats when adding an entry. Set its amount, first date and weekly, monthly or yearly schedule. Free includes three active rules; Premium includes up to 50. Rules and their resulting entries sync to your Cally account.

Cally adds due entries after it has synced; a new rule can add its past due entries straight away. Opening the app online lets it catch up. A day missing from a month uses that month’s last day, then returns to the chosen day in longer months.

Stop a rule to prevent future entries. Entries already added stay. Deleting one occurrence does not stop the whole rule. Delete all entries also stops recurring rules so they do not immediately bring entries back.

Reminders, widgets and quick entries

☰ More → Reminders controls the free daily reminder and Premium bill reminders. Notifications need your permission in iOS. Reminders are planned on the iPhone; they are refreshed as Cally opens and your entries change.

Add Cally widgets using iOS’s widget picker. The Today widget is free; Budget, Month and Lock Screen widgets need Premium. If Cally’s lock is enabled, widget amounts are hidden. Widgets show a local summary rather than connecting to the account server themselves.

With a signed-in Cally account, Siri or Shortcuts can queue an income or expense entry. Cally imports it into that same account when it is open. The queue is cleared on sign-out. Siri availability and phrases depend on Apple’s language and device support.

How do I scan a receipt?

With Premium, open an expense day and choose Scan receipt. Use the document camera on supported iPhones, or select a photo. Recognition runs on your iPhone; Cally does not upload or retain the receipt image.

Check the suggested amount, date and category before you add the expense. Only the entry fields you save sync to your account. You can always enter the amount yourself.

What are safe to spend and the forecast?

Premium uses the entries, recurring rules and budgets you have logged to estimate what is left for today and how the month may end. These figures change with your data; they are not a bank balance or a prediction of unlogged transactions.

Dark mode, language and sharing

Choose Automatic, Light or Dark in ☰ More → Customization. The interface supports English, Greek, Spanish, German, French and Italian; choose the app language in iOS Settings. Apple’s Siri language support is separate.

Use Share your month in Overview to create a recap image. You choose whether it includes amounts and where to share it. Cally does not publish it automatically.

How does Together work?

Open ☰ → Together to create a shared budget or savings goal, or to join with a code. Choose the display name you want the group to see. Only entries added to that group are shared; your personal calendar and email address stay separate.

Creating a group requires Premium. Each member with Premium covers one member without it, and a group needs at least one Premium member. A group can have up to 10 members. If the Premium balance is no longer sufficient, the group keeps its history but cannot accept new entries until the balance is restored.

Share the invite code only with people you want in the group. When you leave, your entries stay under your chosen name; the group’s owner can delete them. If you delete your Cally account, the group entries you added are removed too. The last member leaving deletes the group.

Together needs an internet connection. Personal entries can still be logged offline and sync when you reconnect. Personal JSON and CSV backups do not include Together group data.

Why am I seeing ads?

The free version can show a banner above the tab bar. An occasional full-screen ad can appear after you save an entry and close the day, after your first 24 hours and no more than once every five minutes or three times a day. Premium removes ads.

Cally requests non-personalized ads. It does not send your personal or Together entries, email address, name or Cally account ID to the ad SDK. Google still processes device, advertising, interaction and diagnostic information to provide the ads.

Where required, Google asks for consent before ads can be requested. If applicable, open ☰ More → Privacy & security → Ad privacy choices to revisit those choices. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

Go to ☰ More → Account → Delete account and confirm. This permanently deletes your account and personal entries, along with your Together memberships and the group entries you added. Other iPhones signed in to it are signed out the next time Cally connects on them, and their copies are deleted. If you signed in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm once more. Cally then attempts to revoke its Sign in with Apple access. Your Cally account is deleted even if that revocation fails.

This can’t be undone, so export a backup first if you might want your entries later. Deleting the app doesn’t delete your account. If you sign in again after deleting your account, you start with a new, empty one. Cancel Premium separately through Apple; account deletion does not stop subscription billing.

What happens when I sign out?

Go to ☰ More → Account → Sign out. Cally deletes its copy of your entries from this iPhone. Your account isn’t affected: everything that has synced stays in it, and signing in again brings it back. Your other iPhones stay signed in.

If some changes haven’t synced yet, Cally tries to send them first. If it can’t, for example while you’re offline, it warns you before signing out, because those changes would be lost.

How do I bring in my data from the Cally web app?

  1. In the Cally web app, open Overview, scroll to Backup and tap Save backup. Choose Save to Files, for example to iCloud Drive. (If Safari downloads it instead, it’s in Files → Downloads.)
  2. In the Cally iPhone app, go to ☰ More → Advanced → Import and pick that file.

Your income and expense entries and your income sources come across and are added to your account, so they sync to your other iPhones. Nothing changes in the web app. Import also accepts backups exported from the Cally iPhone app, and importing the same file twice won’t create duplicates.

How do I change the currency?

Go to ☰ More → Money → Currency and pick one. This changes the symbol only. Amounts aren’t converted, so an entry of €50 will show as $50. The currency is saved to your account, so it’s the same on all your iPhones.

How do I turn on the lock?

Go to ☰ More → Privacy & security and turn on Lock with Face ID. On iPhones with Touch ID it says Touch ID; without either, passcode. Confirm once, and from then on Cally asks every time it opens or comes back from the background. It also hides your amounts in the app switcher.

Your iPhone needs a passcode for the lock to work. If Face ID doesn’t recognize you, you can use your iPhone passcode. The lock is set separately on each iPhone. To turn it off, use the same switch.

How do I delete entries?

  • One entry: tap its day, then tap the × next to the entry. You can undo right after. It’s removed on all your iPhones.
  • All entries: go to ☰ More → Advanced → Delete all entries and confirm. This removes every entry and the income sources you added, and stops recurring rules, on all your iPhones, but keeps your account, custom categories, budgets and Together groups. Export a backup first if you might need it.
  • Everything, including your account: see How do I delete my account?

Files you exported are separate copies. Delete those in the Files app.

How do I contact you?

Email support@cally.website. If something isn’t working, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Cally version (shown in ☰ More → About) and how you sign in (Apple, Google or email).

Support messages are forwarded through ImprovMX to my Gmail inbox. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Please don’t attach a backup file unless I ask for one. It contains all your entries.

Still using Cally in your browser? Open the original web app to keep using it or save a backup. Your web entries stay in the browser where you created them.
