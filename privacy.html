Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

In short

  • You sign in so your entries are saved to your account and sync across your iPhones.
  • Your personal entries are used for your account and sync. They are not sent to advertisers or sold.
  • The free version shows Google ads. Premium removes ads. Google processes device, advertising and diagnostic data as described below.
  • Together shares only the group information you choose to add, with people in that group.
  • You can delete your account and all your entries anytime in Settings.

Who I am

Cally is made by Nikos Kaloutsidis, an individual developer in Greece. This policy covers the Cally app for iPhone and the account you use with it.

Under the EU’s data protection law (the GDPR), I’m the controller of your personal data, which means I’m responsible for it. You can reach me at support@cally.website.

What’s stored with your account

Cally needs an account to save and sync your entries. Its server stores this, linked to your account:

  • Your email address. If you sign in with Apple and choose Hide My Email, this is the private relay address Apple gives Cally, not your real one.
  • Your name, only if Apple (on your first sign-in) or Google provides it.
  • Your account ID and which sign-in method you used: Apple, Google or email.
  • Your entries: the date, the amount, whether it’s expected income, unexpected income or an expense, and any category, income source or payment method you choose. The server also records when each entry was added and last changed, and its order within the day.
  • Your settings: your currency, income sources, custom expense categories, and any monthly or category budgets you set.
  • Together, if you use it: the group’s name, whether it is a shared budget or savings goal, its target amount and currency, invite code, memberships and chosen display names, and the date, amount, optional note and author of each group entry. Group members can also see each member’s Premium status.
  • Premium, if you subscribe: the subscription product, renewal or expiry information, and App Store transaction identifiers. A purchase carries your Cally account ID so the server can link the subscription to your account. Apple handles billing; Cally does not receive your card details.

The sign-in system also keeps a few details it needs to run your account:

  • When you signed up and last signed in.
  • For each iPhone where you’re signed in, the IP address and the app and iOS version it last connected with. These go when that iPhone signs out, or when you delete your account.
  • If you use Google, your Google account ID and the link to your Google profile picture, which Cally never shows.
  • If you sign in with Apple, a token from Apple, used only to revoke Cally’s access if you delete your account.

For security, the sign-in system and Supabase also keep logs, such as IP addresses and request times. Cally schedules its sign-in audit records for deletion daily once they are more than 7 days old, normally removing them within 8 days. When you delete your account, Cally also requests deletion of your associated sign-in audit records; cleanup failures can delay removal. Supabase’s other operational logs follow its platform retention settings.

Cally does not ask for a password, your phone number, contacts, photos, bank details or permission to access your location. Google’s ad software may estimate a general area from an IP address, as explained below.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Whether the Face ID or passcode lock is on.
  • When you last exported a backup, and your chosen tab layout, app icon and appearance.
  • A cached Premium status, kept separately for your Cally account, and a small on-device record of up to three active calendar days, whether you closed the paid plans and whether the optional trial was shown. This trial-presentation record is kept per account on that device and is not sent to Cally or advertisers.
  • A copy of your entries, so Cally opens and works offline. Changes you make offline sync when you’re back online.
  • Your sign-in session, in the iPhone’s Keychain, so you stay signed in.
  • Google’s ad-consent settings. Google also processes the consent signals needed to honour your choices.

The lock setting, export date, tab layout and appearance choices are not sent to Cally’s server.

Where it’s stored

Your account and entries are stored and processed on Cally’s server, which runs on Supabase in the EU, in Frankfurt, Germany (Amazon Web Services, eu-central-1). For account storage and sync, the app connects to Cally’s Supabase project over HTTPS and requests the same region for server-function calls.

The app also connects to Apple for Premium purchases and to Google’s ad and consent services in the free version. These services may process data outside the EU. When you sign in with Apple or Google, you do it on their own sign-in screens. Privacy and support links open in Safari.

Services Cally uses

  • Supabase runs Cally’s database, sign-in and server functions, and stores your data in Frankfurt.
  • Resend sends the email sign-in codes, so it handles your email address.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple and Premium billing, under Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Google handles Sign in with Google and provides the Google AdMob advertising and consent software in the free version. See Google’s privacy policy and its ad SDK data disclosures.
  • ImprovMX forwards messages sent to the support email address.
  • Gmail, operated by Google, receives and stores support correspondence.

Some of these companies may process data outside the EU. When they do, the transfer is covered by safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Ads and your choices

The free version can show a Google AdMob banner above the tab bar and an occasional full-screen ad after you save an entry and close the day. Full-screen ads do not appear in your first 24 hours and are limited to one every five minutes and three a day. Premium removes these ad placements.

  • What Google processes. Google’s ad and consent software may process your IP address and the general area it suggests, device and app information and identifiers, ads shown and tapped, product interactions, consent signals, and crash, performance and diagnostic information. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, operate its services and prevent fraud.
  • Your money stays out of ads. Cally does not pass your personal entries, Together group entries, email address, name or Cally account ID to the ad SDK. Ads are not selected from your financial entries.
  • Non-personalized requests. Cally requests non-personalized ads and does not ask for Apple’s permission to access the advertising identifier. This does not mean that the ad SDK collects no data; the processing above can still occur.
  • Your choices. Where required, Google presents a consent form before ads can be requested. If Google requires a way to revisit those choices, Cally shows Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can change your choices there.

Cally has no separate analytics service of its own. The Google SDK’s advertising and diagnostic processing follows Google’s policies and the choices available in its consent form.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple handles Premium payments and stores its own purchase records. Cally checks the subscription on the device and keeps a server record of its plan and validity so it can work with your Cally account on another iPhone.

A free trial is available only when Apple confirms that you are eligible. Each subscription group allows one introductory offer per person. The App Store shows the applicable price, trial and renewal terms before you confirm a purchase.

To cancel, use Settings → Manage subscription in Cally or your iPhone’s Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Deleting Cally or your Cally account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

How it’s used

Cally uses account data to provide your account, sync, optional Together groups and Premium access, and security logs to protect the service. Under the GDPR, the legal basis for these account services is performance of a contract: Cally needs this data to provide the features you choose to use. If you contact support, I also use the information you send to answer your request.

Google processes advertising and consent data for the free version as described above, with consent where required. Cally does not sell your personal financial entries or use them to target advertising.

Who can see it

You, on every iPhone where you’re signed in, and anyone who can unlock and use those iPhones. To keep it from people who use your phone, turn on the lock.

If you create or join a Together group, its members can see that group’s details, chosen display names, membership and Premium status, and group entries. Your personal income and expense calendar and email address are not shared with the group. Share an invite code only with people you want to invite.

As the developer, I can access Cally’s server to operate and support these services. The service providers listed above process the data needed for their roles. I do not sell your account data.

iCloud backup

Cally keeps its copy of your entries out of iCloud and computer backups, because your account already holds them. iOS still backs up Cally’s few settings, such as whether the lock is on, and your sign-in session, so an iPhone restored from a backup can stay signed in. iCloud backups are kept in your iCloud account under Apple’s privacy policy, and I can’t access them.

Face ID and passcode lock

The lock is optional, stays off until you turn it on, and is set separately on each iPhone. iOS does the checking, using Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. Your biometric data never leaves Apple’s Secure Enclave and is never seen by Cally; the app only learns whether unlocking worked. Cally never sees your passcode either.

Exported backups

When you tap Export in Settings, Cally creates a JSON file with your personal entries and settings and saves it wherever you choose in the Files app. Premium can also export your personal entries as a CSV spreadsheet. These exports do not include Together group data. Neither file is encrypted. Once it’s saved, it’s yours: anyone you share it with can read it, and if you keep it in iCloud Drive or another cloud service, that service’s terms apply. Import reads the file you pick and adds its entries to your account, so they sync like any others.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account, personal entries and settings from Cally’s server, right away, along with your Together memberships and the group entries you added. Other iPhones signed in to the account are signed out the next time Cally connects on them (within an hour while it’s open), and their copies are deleted then. If you signed in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm once more. Cally then attempts to revoke its Sign in with Apple access. Your Cally account is deleted even if that revocation fails.
  • Settings → Delete all entries removes your personal entries and the income sources you added, on all your iPhones. It keeps your account, custom categories, budgets and Together groups.
  • Leaving a Together group removes your membership. The entries you added stay under the display name you used, so the group’s totals still make sense; its owner can delete them. The last member leaving deletes the group and all its entries.
  • Signing out deletes the copy of your account data on that iPhone. Your account stays.
  • Deleting the app removes Cally’s copy from your iPhone, but not your account. To delete your account, use Settings → Delete account first.

When you delete an entry, one at a time or all at once, the server erases its date, amount, type, category, source and payment method when the deletion reaches it. Deletions made offline are sent when Cally reconnects. The server keeps only the entry’s ID and the time it was deleted, so your other iPhones know to remove it, and that goes too when you delete your account. Any copies in Supabase’s backups follow its backup retention settings.

Files you exported are separate copies. Delete those in the Files app. Cancel any Premium subscription separately through Apple; deleting your account does not stop subscription billing.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can:

  • See your data. Your entries are in the app. Email me for a copy of everything stored with your account.
  • Correct it. Delete a wrong entry and add it again, or email me to correct anything else, such as your name.
  • Delete it. Use Settings → Delete account.
  • Take it with you. Settings → Export saves your personal entries and settings as a JSON file. Premium can also export a CSV spreadsheet.
  • Restrict or object to how it’s used. Email me.
  • Complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority at www.dpa.gr.

Children

Cally isn’t meant for children under 16.

Apple and this website

If you chose to share analytics with app developers on your iPhone, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

The primary Cally website is hosted with OpenAI Sites. An existing copy of these pages and the original Cally web app are hosted on GitHub Pages (see GitHub’s privacy statement). These hosting providers may process visitors’ IP addresses and request information to deliver and secure the pages. This applies to the websites only, not to the iPhone app.

Changes

If this policy changes, I’ll update this page and the effective date above. If Cally starts collecting other kinds of data, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or want to use one of your rights? Email support@cally.website.

Messages and attachments sent there are forwarded by ImprovMX to my Gmail inbox, operated by Google. ImprovMX processes them for forwarding, and Google processes and stores them as Gmail messages. These services may retain delivery or security records under their own policies. I use this correspondence to answer you and handle your request. Support emails are separate from your Cally account; deleting your account does not automatically delete this correspondence.
