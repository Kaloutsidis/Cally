Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

In short

  • You sign in so your entries are saved to your account and sync across your iPhones.
  • Your account data is used for accounts, sync and security. No ads, no tracking, never sold.
  • You can delete your account and all your entries anytime in Settings.

Who I am

Cally is made by Nikos Kaloutsidis, an individual developer in Greece. This policy covers the Cally app for iPhone and the account you use with it.

Under the EU’s data protection law (the GDPR), I’m the controller of your personal data, which means I’m responsible for it. You can reach me at support@cally.website.

What’s stored with your account

Cally needs an account to save and sync your entries. Its server stores this, linked to your account:

  • Your email address. If you sign in with Apple and choose Hide My Email, this is the private relay address Apple gives Cally, not your real one.
  • Your name, only if Apple (on your first sign-in) or Google provides it.
  • Your account ID and which sign-in method you used: Apple, Google or email.
  • Your entries: the date, the amount, whether it’s expected income, unexpected income or an expense, and any category, income source or payment method you choose. The server also records when each entry was added and last changed, and its order within the day.
  • Your settings: your currency and your list of income sources.

The sign-in system also keeps a few details it needs to run your account:

  • When you signed up and last signed in.
  • For each iPhone where you’re signed in, the IP address and the app and iOS version it last connected with. These go when that iPhone signs out, or when you delete your account.
  • If you use Google, your Google account ID and the link to your Google profile picture, which Cally never shows.
  • If you sign in with Apple, a token from Apple, used only to revoke Cally’s access if you delete your account.

For security, the sign-in system and Supabase also keep logs, such as IP addresses and request times. Cally schedules its sign-in audit records for deletion daily once they are more than 7 days old, normally removing them within 8 days. When you delete your account, Cally also requests deletion of your associated sign-in audit records; cleanup failures can delay removal. Supabase’s other operational logs follow its platform retention settings.

Cally never asks for a password, your phone number, location, contacts, photos or bank details.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Whether the Face ID or passcode lock is on.
  • When you last exported a backup.
  • A copy of your entries, so Cally opens and works offline. Changes you make offline sync when you’re back online.
  • Your sign-in session, in the iPhone’s Keychain, so you stay signed in.

The lock setting and the export date are never sent to the server.

Where it’s stored

Your account and entries are stored and processed on Cally’s server, which runs on Supabase in the EU, in Frankfurt, Germany (Amazon Web Services, eu-central-1). For account storage and sync, the app connects to Cally’s Supabase project over HTTPS and requests the same region for server-function calls.

When you sign in with Apple or Google, you do it on Apple’s or Google’s own sign-in screen. The privacy policy and support links open in Safari.

Services Cally uses

  • Supabase runs Cally’s database, sign-in and server functions, and stores your data in Frankfurt.
  • Resend sends the email sign-in codes, so it handles your email address.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple, if you use it.
  • Google handles Sign in with Google, if you use it.
  • ImprovMX forwards messages sent to the support email address.
  • Gmail, operated by Google, receives and stores support correspondence.

Some of these companies may process data outside the EU. When they do, the transfer is covered by safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How it’s used

Cally uses account data to provide your account and keep your entries in sync, and security logs to protect the service. Under the GDPR, the legal basis for the account and sync is performance of a contract: Cally needs this data to provide the service you signed up for. If you contact support, I also use the information you send to answer your request.

Cally has no ads, analytics or tracking. It doesn’t profile you, doesn’t sell your data, and has no third-party SDKs for advertising or marketing.

Who can see it

You, on every iPhone where you’re signed in, and anyone who can unlock and use those iPhones. To keep it from people who use your phone, turn on the lock.

No other Cally user can see your data. As the developer, I can access Cally’s server to keep it running, but I use your data only to provide your account and sync. Apart from the services above, I don’t share it with anyone.

iCloud backup

Cally keeps its copy of your entries out of iCloud and computer backups, because your account already holds them. iOS still backs up Cally’s few settings, such as whether the lock is on, and your sign-in session, so an iPhone restored from a backup can stay signed in. iCloud backups are kept in your iCloud account under Apple’s privacy policy, and I can’t access them.

Face ID and passcode lock

The lock is optional, stays off until you turn it on, and is set separately on each iPhone. iOS does the checking, using Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. Your biometric data never leaves Apple’s Secure Enclave and is never seen by Cally; the app only learns whether unlocking worked. Cally never sees your passcode either.

Exported backups

When you tap Export in Settings, Cally creates a JSON file with your entries and income sources and saves it wherever you choose in the Files app. The file is not encrypted. Once it’s saved, it’s yours: anyone you share it with can read it, and if you keep it in iCloud Drive or another cloud service, that service’s terms apply. Import reads the file you pick and adds its entries to your account, so they sync like any others.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account, entries and settings from Cally’s server, right away. Other iPhones signed in to the account are signed out the next time Cally connects on them (within an hour while it’s open), and their copies are deleted then. If you signed in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm once more. Cally then attempts to revoke its Sign in with Apple access. Your Cally account is deleted even if that revocation fails.
  • Settings → Delete all entries removes every entry and the income sources you added, on all your iPhones, and keeps your account.
  • Signing out deletes the copy of your data on that iPhone. Your account stays.
  • Deleting the app removes Cally’s copy from your iPhone, but not your account. To delete your account, use Settings → Delete account first.

When you delete an entry, one at a time or all at once, the server erases its date, amount, type, category, source and payment method when the deletion reaches it. Deletions made offline are sent when Cally reconnects. The server keeps only the entry’s ID and the time it was deleted, so your other iPhones know to remove it, and that goes too when you delete your account. Any copies in Supabase’s backups follow its backup retention settings.

Files you exported are separate copies. Delete those in the Files app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can:

  • See your data. Your entries are in the app. Email me for a copy of everything stored with your account.
  • Correct it. Delete a wrong entry and add it again, or email me to correct anything else, such as your name.
  • Delete it. Use Settings → Delete account.
  • Take it with you. Settings → Export saves your entries and income sources as a JSON file.
  • Restrict or object to how it’s used. Email me.
  • Complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority at www.dpa.gr.

Children

Cally isn’t meant for children under 16.

Apple and this website

If you chose to share analytics with app developers on your iPhone, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

The primary Cally website is hosted with OpenAI Sites. An existing copy of these pages and the original Cally web app are hosted on GitHub Pages (see GitHub’s privacy statement). These hosting providers may process visitors’ IP addresses and request information to deliver and secure the pages. This applies to the websites only, not to the iPhone app.

Changes

If this policy changes, I’ll update this page and the effective date above. If Cally starts collecting other kinds of data, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or want to use one of your rights? Email support@cally.website.

Messages and attachments sent there are forwarded by ImprovMX to my Gmail inbox, operated by Google. ImprovMX processes them for forwarding, and Google processes and stores them as Gmail messages. These services may retain delivery or security records under their own policies. I use this correspondence to answer you and handle your request. Support emails are separate from your Cally account; deleting your account does not automatically delete this correspondence.
